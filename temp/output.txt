First we calculate the total resistance between the generator nodes (VS1)
R12 and R11 connected in parallel so Rc=R12*R11/(R12+R11)=2Ohm*2Ohm/(2Ohm+2Ohm)=1Ohm
Rc and R10 connected in series so Rb=Rc+R10=1Ohm+1Ohm=2Ohm
R9 and R8 connected in parallel so Rh=R9*R8/(R9+R8)=2Ohm*2Ohm/(2Ohm+2Ohm)=1Ohm
Rh and R7 connected in series so Rg=Rh+R7=1Ohm+1Ohm=2Ohm
R4 and R5 connected in series so Rk=R4+R5=1Ohm+1Ohm=2Ohm
R2 and R1 connected in parallel so Rn=R2*R1/(R2+R1)=2Ohm*2Ohm/(2Ohm+2Ohm)=1Ohm
Rn and R3 connected in series so Rm=Rn+R3=1Ohm+1Ohm=2Ohm
Rk and Rm connected in parallel so Rj=Rk*Rm/(Rk+Rm)=2Ohm*2Ohm/(2Ohm+2Ohm)=1Ohm
Rg and Rj connected in series so Rf=Rg+Rj=2Ohm+1Ohm=3Ohm
Rf and R6 connected in series so Re=Rf+R6=3Ohm+1Ohm=4Ohm
Rb and Re connected in parallel so Ra=Rb*Re/(Rb+Re)=2Ohm*4Ohm/(2Ohm+4Ohm)=1.33Ohm
The total resistance is Ra = 1.33Ohm
