First we calculate the total resistance between the generator nodes (Is)
R1 and R2 connected in series so Rb=R1+R2=10Ohm+30Ohm=40Ohm
R and Rb connected in parallel so Ra=R*Rb/(R+Rb)=10Ohm*40Ohm/(10Ohm+40Ohm)=8Ohm
The total resistance is Ra = 8Ohm

Now we calculate the voltage on VM1
We know that the voltage between Is nodes is 16 Volt, because the generator current is Igen = 2A and the resistance between the generator nodes is Rtot = 8Ohm so the voltage between the generator nodes is Igen*Rtot = 16 Volt
